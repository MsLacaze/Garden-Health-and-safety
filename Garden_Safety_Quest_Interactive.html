MISSION 1 · KNOW YOUR KIT

🧰 The Safety Toolkit

Before you begin, learn what each safety rule protects you from. Open each card and discuss why it matters.

👂 Follow instructions

Listen to the teacher's instructions before starting. Check what the task is, which tools to use, and where to work. If you are unsure, stop and ask.

👀 Look around

Check for uneven ground, hoses, tools, branches, insects and other people. Walk—don't rush—and leave enough space for others to work safely.

☀️ Sun and water

Wear a hat, use sunscreen and drink water on hot days. Take a break and tell the teacher if you feel dizzy, sick or overheated.

🛠️ Use tools properly

Use each tool only for its intended job. Carry it carefully, keep the working end under control, and never play or wave tools around.

📍 Put tools away

Return tools to the correct storage area after use. Tools left on the ground can cause trips, cuts or damage to the tool.

🧤 Protect your body

Wear closed-toe shoes and any protective clothing required for the task. Tie long hair back. Use gloves when needed for thorns, rough materials or dirty work.

😷 Compost and potting mix

Follow the teacher's instructions and product guidance. Avoid breathing dust: work gently, keep your face away, and use a suitable mask when instructed. Wash hands afterwards.

🧼 Clean hands

Wash your hands after handling soil, compost, plants or chemicals, and before eating or drinking. This helps reduce the spread of germs and contamination.

🌱 Know your plants

Some plants have thorns, irritating sap or can trigger allergic reactions. Don't taste unknown plants or touch unfamiliar sap. Ask before handling an unknown plant.

🩹 Speak up

Tell the teacher straight away about cuts, bites, stings, rashes, breathing problems or other injuries. Don't hide an injury or assume it will be fine.

🧪 Chemicals and fertilisers

Only use them when the teacher has approved the task. Follow the label and school instructions, wear the required protection, and never mix products or touch them casually.

🦺 Dress for the task

Wear closed-toe shoes, a hi-vis vest where required, gloves or other protective gear as directed. Secure loose clothing and jewellery that could catch on tools.

MISSION 4 · REAL-LIFE DECISIONS

🧠 Garden Safety Scenarios

Make a decision for each situation. Try to explain your reasoning to a partner before revealing the suggested response.

Scenario A: The mystery plant

You see a plant with shiny leaves and unusual berries. Your friend says, “Try one and see what it tastes like!”

Safe response: Don't eat it or touch it unnecessarily. Unknown plants may be poisonous or irritating. Keep others away and ask the teacher to identify it.

Scenario B: The small cut

You scratch your hand on a thorn. It looks tiny, so you think about carrying on without telling anyone.

Safe response: Stop and tell the teacher. Follow the school's first-aid procedure. Reporting injuries promptly means the wound can be checked and treated properly.

Scenario C: The dusty potting mix

A classmate empties a dry bag of potting mix quickly. Dust rises into the air and you are standing nearby.

Safe response: Move away from the dust and alert the teacher. Follow the product guidance and class instructions, use safe handling methods and the required suitable mask. Avoid creating or breathing dust.

Scenario D: The unknown chemical

You find an unlabelled bottle beside the garden shed. Someone says it is probably fertiliser and asks you to use it.

Safe response: Do not open, smell, touch or use it. Keep people away and tell the teacher. Chemicals must be identified and handled according to their label and school procedures.

Scenario E: Feeling unwell

It is hot, you forgot your hat, and you feel dizzy while digging.

Safe response: Stop working, move to a safe shaded area, tell the teacher immediately and follow their instructions. Drink water if you can do so safely. Do not try to push through dizziness.
